  • CVPR 2026IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition

    TTRV: Test-Time Reinforcement Learning for Vision Language Models

    Akshit Singh, Shyam Marjit, Wei Lin, Paul Gavrikov, Serena Yeung-Levy, Hilde Kuehne, Rogerio Feris, Sivan Doveh, James Glass, M. Jehanzeb Mirza

    TTRV adapts a vision-language model during inference with reinforcement learning, using rewards computed from the model’s own outputs: how often an answer appears, and how diverse the answers are. No labels are needed, it works even from a single unlabeled test sample, and across 15+ multimodal benchmarks it lifts open-source models past GPT-4o by 2%.

  • ACCV 202417th Asian Conference on Computer Vision
    Oral, 5.6% acceptanceFirst author

    CCNDF: Curvature Constrained Neural Distance Fields from 3D LiDAR Sequences

    Akshit Singh, Karan Bhakuni, Rajendra Nagar

    Neural distance fields struggle on curved, complex geometry. CCNDF uses second-order derivatives of the field to estimate the radius of curvature and constrains learning with it, giving better mapping and localization from LiDAR sequences.

    Advancing Connected Vehicle Security with Comprehensive Datasets and Novel Anomaly Detection and Recovery Techniques

    Akshit Singh, Heena Rathore

    A reproducible dataset built by interpolating the U.S. DOT Tampa connected-vehicle data with simulated sensor faults (white and Lévy noise, bias, spikes), plus a framework that detects and recovers from anomalies in real time with Bayesian online changepoint detection and Bayesian forecasting.

    Deep Dense Clothed Genus-0 Human 3D Shape Matching using Spherical Harmonics

    Deepanshu Solanki*, Akshit Singh*, Rajendra Nagar

    Clothed human meshes are mapped onto spheres with Möbius transformations, turning a non-rigid problem into a rigid one. Graph neural networks and spherical harmonics then produce features that a functional-map solver uses for dense correspondence.

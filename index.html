About

I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at Dartmouth College. Before that, I earned my undergraduate degree at IIT Jodhpur. Along the way, I've interned at MIT CSAIL, Texas State University, HyperVerge and IIT Jodhpur, each one adding a new piece to how I think about research.

My work has been published at CVPR and AAAI, and I had the honor of presenting an oral at ACCV. I've also worked as an independent researcher, and I loved the freedom to choose my own questions and follow them wherever they led.

Above all, I care about problems that affect people and society. The interests below come from my work so far, but they will keep changing as I find new problems worth solving.

  • Test-time learning for AIModels that adapt and improve themselves at inference, without ground-truth labels.
  • Long-context multimodal reasoningReasoning across long sequences of images, video and text.
  • Active vision and roboticsGeneralizable robots, test-time learning for robotics, implicit surfaces and LiDAR mapping.
